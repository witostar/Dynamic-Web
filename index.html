How the data shapes your view

🪟 The view The ISS's live position slides the real Earth across your window.

🌗 Day & night When the ISS enters Earth's shadow, the view fades to city lights, the page goes dark and the hum gets deeper.

🏠 Distance from home The closer the ISS gets to your home, the warmer the glow, the fuller the bar and the louder the hum.

🗺️ Place A second API turns the coordinates into the name of the ocean or country below you.

🌅 Events A message pops up at every sunrise and sunset in space (about 16 a day).
